## 2. PROPOSED CONTENT MODIFICATION COMPARISON MATRIX

### Section: Homepage Hero
- **Existing Content**: "Regal Cultural Authority & Unrivalled Ceremonial Elegance"
- **Current Content Problem**: Needed strong supporting statement clarifying geographical reach (UK, Europe, Nigeria, Destination) and target audience.
- **Recommended Content**: "Royal ceremonial hosting, traditional Alaga engagement direction, and high-energy luxury Master of Ceremonies for discerning couples across the UK, Nigeria, Europe, and international destination celebrations."
- **Reason for Change**: Establishes immediate clarity, credibility, and geographical authority.
- **SEO Purpose**: Targets "Yoruba wedding MC UK", "Alaga engagement direction", and "international destination celebrations".
- **Conversion Purpose**: Immediately signals capacity to host high-value international events and encourages booking inquiries.

---

### Section: Services Overview (Alaga Iduro vs Alaga Ijoko)
- **Existing Content**: Basic short card descriptions.
- **Current Content Problem**: Potential confusion for first-time couples or non-Yoruba wedding planners regarding who represents which family.
- **Recommended Content**: Clear role definitions: Alaga Iduro = Groom's Family Spokesperson; Alaga Ijoko = Bride's Family Host.
- **Reason for Change**: Eliminates confusion and builds immediate cultural confidence.
- **SEO Purpose**: Captures high-intent searches for "Alaga Iduro groom family" and "Alaga Ijoko bride family".
- **Conversion Purpose**: Enables couples to select the exact service required without hesitation.

---

### Section: Testimonials & Credibility
- **Existing Content**: Generic placeholder text.
- **Current Content Problem**: Must avoid fake names, fabricated client reviews, or manufactured awards.
- **Recommended Content**: Explicitly marked placeholders: `[VERIFIED CLIENT TESTIMONIAL REQUIRED]` and `[VERIFIED AWARD REQUIRED]`.
- **Reason for Change**: Upholds strict brand integrity and anti-fabrication guidelines.
- **SEO Purpose**: Maintains semantic structure ready for live client reviews.
- **Conversion Purpose**: Protects brand trust by guaranteeing zero fake claims.
