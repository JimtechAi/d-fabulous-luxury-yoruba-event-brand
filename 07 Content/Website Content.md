## 3. INDIVIDUAL SERVICE DETAILS

### Service 1: Alaga Iduro
- **Title**: Alaga Iduro (Groom's Family Spokesperson)
- **Category**: Core Ceremonial Service
- **Definition**: In Yoruba traditional engagements, the Alaga Iduro represents the groom's family. They are responsible for formally requesting the bride's hand in marriage, presenting the groom's delegation with respect, and navigating ceremonial negotiations with grace and humor.
- **What D’Fabulous Delivers**:
  - Fluent Yoruba and English ceremonial negotiation.
  - Respectful execution of traditional prostration (Dobale) protocols for the groom and groomsmen.
  - Presentation of official engagement letters and family requests.
  - Harmonious interaction with the bride's family Alaga (Alaga Ijoko).

---

### Service 2: Alaga Ijoko
- **Title**: Alaga Ijoko (Bride's Family Host)
- **Category**: Core Ceremonial Service
- **Definition**: The Alaga Ijoko acts as the official host and custodian for the bride's family during the traditional engagement. They guide the proceedings, receive the groom's family, oversee the inspection and acceptance of the Eru Iyawo (dowry gifts), and usher in the bride.
- **What D’Fabulous Delivers**:
  - Authoritative ceremonial host who maintains order, warmth, and dignity.
  - Traditional gift inspection and ceremonial unveiling protocols.
  - Emotional and joyful unveiling of the bride (Iyawos entrance).
  - Parental blessings management and ancestral family recognition.
